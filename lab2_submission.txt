CSCI 6201 Lab 2 submission

Project member: Shamir Tyner
Dataset: County Presidential Election Returns, 2000–2024
GitHub repository: https://github.com/shamirtyner96/6201-final-project
Dataset version: The original dataset is used. The analysis filters it to Democratic and Republican TOTAL records without changing the source file.

Research question: Are swing states really swinging, or are they shifting? Do competitive states show repeated election-to-election swings or longer-term shifts in their two-party presidential vote margins?
