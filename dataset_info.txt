Dataset: County Presidential Election Returns, 2000–2024
Source: MIT Election Data + Science Lab, Harvard Dataverse
URL: https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/VOQCHQ
Description: County and county-equivalent presidential election returns by year, candidate, party, and reporting mode.
Version used: The original CSV is retained in the repository. The analysis filters to Democratic and Republican TOTAL records, omits localities without a record for both parties, and derives two-party results from the remaining records; it does not alter the CSV.
