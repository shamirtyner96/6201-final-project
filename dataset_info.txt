Dataset information

Name: County Presidential Election Returns, 2000–2024
Source: MIT Election Data + Science Lab, Harvard Dataverse
Access: https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/VOQCHQ

Description: Presidential election vote totals reported by county or county-equivalent, election year, candidate, party, and voting mode.

Version used: The repository contains the original CSV. The analysis uses only Democratic and Republican records marked TOTAL and adds their votes by state and year. It does not alter the original data. Missing TOTAL records may result in unavailable or partial state margins.
